About

A calm place to read, focus and take a break.

Bookworm Study Club is designed for people who want a comfortable place to read, study independently, and step away when they need to. Whether you're preparing for an exam, working on a project, or simply enjoying a book, the club offers a quiet, well-maintained environment.

We have a library, a small café, a prayer space, and plenty of seating. No classes, no noise — just a good place to get things done.
